Read AGENTS.md and docs/ARCHITECTURE_INDEX.md. Resolve the requested symbol,
mode, run and service owner from available evidence. Do not substitute the
current configuration or present-day inputs for missing historical values.

Follow the real producer and consumer of reason, gate, direction and attempts.
Distinguish failed planning candidates, feature diagnostic occurrences and
terminal rejected symbols; their counts do not necessarily partition a universe.
Use current trace mappings, but verify that their source buffer belongs to the
requested paper/live/scanner service before claiming an end-to-end trace.

Inspect existing records read-only with explicit provenance. Do not start a bot,
fetch external data, modify journals, run unguarded imports or invent a side.
Report the earliest evidenced failure, known inputs/settings, downstream effects
and missing evidence. Unknown is a valid result, not grounds to guess.
